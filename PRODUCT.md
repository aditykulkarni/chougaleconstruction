# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`, with no build step. Three.js r128 from cdnjs for the 3D scenes.

## Users

A mix of people around Kolhapur district who are deciding who should build for them. Families and landowners come first: someone with a plot who wants a house, a bungalow or an apartment building and needs a contractor they can trust with their savings. Shop owners, commercial landlords and small industrial units (for example at Shiroli MIDC) also visit. All of them matter equally, and many will look on a phone, often after hearing the name from someone local.

## Product Purpose

The website for Chougale Constructions (चौगले कन्स्ट्रक्शन्स), a family construction firm based in Shinganapur, Karvir, near Kolhapur. It exists to make a visitor trust the firm enough to call, WhatsApp or ask for a site visit. Success means a qualified enquiry about a real plot.

## Positioning

More than 30 years of trust under one family name. On-time handover. The firm's own site team, not subcontracted labour. One person answerable from the plot to the keys.

## Operating Context

Visitors weigh the decision with their family and compare against other local contractors and word of mouth. They respond to finished buildings they can recognise, places they know in the district, and a person they can call. The firm works in Marathi and English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site needs: the company story, flagship projects, where the firm builds (its footprint across Kolhapur district), and contact. It must work well on both phone and desktop.
- Earlier choices Aditya made that still stand: the story told floor by floor on a building, a before/after view for projects, and a 3D map of the district.
- Bilingual: English and Marathi.
- The firm builds homes, bungalows, apartment buildings, commercial buildings and industrial sheds.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: always चौगले कन्स्ट्रक्शन्स / Chougale Constructions.
- Logo: Option F, Slab Monogram (a C built from concrete slabs, with a gold top slab), bilingual. Files are in `assets/` and `/mnt/project-files/branding/logo/`. Clear space equals the gold slab height. The horizontal logo is at least 240px wide. Never recolour, retype, fade, or place it on gold.
- Brand guidelines: https://claude.ai/artifact/QSXUnwx2L4cUy4Zr3zZRhH
  - Colours: charcoal #1E1A17, cream #F2EDE4, haldi gold #E8A50C, stone grey #645D55. Never use gold text on a light background.
  - Fonts: Big Shoulders Display in capitals for English headings. Mukta for all Marathi and body text. Never letter-space Marathi.
- Aditya rejected two earlier versions as looking like "AI slop".
- Aditya shared a dark, photo-led architecture site as a reference for the feel they like.

## Evidence on Hand

Aditya has real project photos, a list of finished projects, the founding year, figures, phone, WhatsApp and address, and will share them once a direction is chosen. Until then, every project name, figure, year, number and photo on the site is a placeholder and must be labelled as one. No testimonials, awards or counts may be invented. The firm has more than 30 years of history, so the placeholder founding year of 2004 is wrong.

## Product Principles

- Trust before spectacle. Every section should make the firm more believable, not just more impressive.
- Show real buildings, real places and a real person to call.
- Make the family name and the decades of work visible.
- Speak to a local family as well as a business owner, in Marathi and English.
- Contacting the firm must be one tap away, especially on a phone.
